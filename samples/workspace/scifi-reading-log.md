# Reading log — sci-fi for structural inspiration

Finished "The Three-Body Problem" last night. What strikes me is the
description of a civilization that treats information as the scarcest
resource — every transmission across interstellar space is a bet about
whether the receiver still exists.

Also came across a random Wikipedia article about knowledge graphs, which
frames entities and relations as a continuously extended map of what a
community knows. Same instinct as the novel: memory is infrastructure, and
whoever controls the index controls what can be connected later.

The writing project needs this structure — chapters that remember each other
across three hundred pages without the reader noticing the wiring.
